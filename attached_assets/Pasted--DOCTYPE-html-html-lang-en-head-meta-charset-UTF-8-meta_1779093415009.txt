    <h1>Bill of Materials (BOM) — Puri & Aloo Charchari</h1>
    <p>Estimated requirements to prepare Puri and Aloo Charchari (Bengali style simple potato stir-fry/gravy) for <strong>100 people</strong>, calculated at <strong>4 pieces of puri per person</strong> (Total: 400 puris).</p>

    <h2>4. Packing & Operations (If Catering/Sourcing)</h2>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Water:</strong> Approximately 5 to 6 liters for kneading dough; 6 to 8 liters for cooking the potatoes.</li>
        <li><strong>Dry Atta for Dusting:</strong> ~500 grams (if rolling out using dry flour instead of oil).</li>
    </ul>

    <div class="blockquote">
        <p><strong>💡 Catering Tip:</strong> It is always safe to keep a <strong>10% buffer</strong> when catering for exactly 100 guests. Bumping the flour to 12 kg and potatoes to 20 kg will safely cover any extra demand or heavy eaters.</p>
